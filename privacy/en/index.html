3. Why we use it

  • To run the app: store your transactions, sync them between iPhone, Apple Watch, and widgets, and show statistics, budgets, and goals.
  • To build weekly, monthly, and annual reports and briefings about your finances.
  • To send the notifications you turned on: scheduled-payment reminders, report-ready alerts, Apple Pay transaction confirmations.
  • To recognise receipts, statements, and voice commands and turn them into transactions, and to produce the written analysis in reports — only with your consent to AI processing (section 4).
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing profiling, and we do not share it with data brokers.

4. Artificial-intelligence features

Some Monni features rely on external AI models. The data listed below is sent only with your explicit consent. You give it when you sign in: the text directly under the sign-in button states that data for AI features is sent to OpenAI and Google. If you withdraw consent, the app offers it again before you use an AI feature. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → AI data sharing. With consent, data is sent when you use the AI features and when the app automatically produces a weekly, monthly, or annual report; automatic report generation can be turned off separately in the app settings. Part of the recognition runs on your device and is never sent anywhere. The app remains fully usable without the AI features: manual entry, analytics, budgets, goals, scheduled payments, and the debt book work without contacting any external model.

Features that use AI: smart input (parsing text and voice commands into transactions), receipt scanning, bank-statement import, bill splitting, chat with the assistant, written insights in reports and briefings, budget suggestions.

What is sent: the text of your request or command, the receipt photo or statement text, and the data needed for the analysis — transactions for the period, balances, categories, currency. Data goes to our server and from there to the AI provider.

To whom: OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C., USA). Google Gemini (Google LLC, USA) may be used as a fallback model. Under these providers' API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models. API keys for AI providers are stored only on our server and never inside the app.

Important: AI output can contain mistakes. Monni's insights and suggestions are for information only and are not professional financial, tax, or legal advice.

6. Where and how long data is stored

  • On the server. Account and financial data are stored in a Supabase database in Singapore for as long as your account exists.
  • On the device. The app keeps a local copy for offline use and a shared snapshot for widgets and Apple Watch (in a protected App Group container). This data is protected by iOS system encryption and is removed when the app is deleted.
  • After account deletion. All data is deleted from the server immediately and irreversibly (section 8). The provider's technical backups are purged within 30 days.
  • AI requests. We do not keep requests sent to the AI provider longer than needed to obtain the response, except for the finished report texts, which are saved in your account.

7. How we protect data

  • All data is transferred over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • Server-side access is restricted at the database-row level: each user can only see their own records.
  • Session keys are stored in the device's system Keychain.
  • AI-provider keys and database service keys are never embedded in the app.
  • Account deletion requires a valid session and typed confirmation.

8. Your rights and controls

  • Access and correction. All your data can be viewed and edited in the app.
  • Export. Settings → Export transactions (CSV).
  • Withdraw AI consent. Settings → AI data sharing. After that no data is sent to AI providers, and the app keeps working without the AI features.
  • Automatic reports. Turn off in the app settings — after that no report data is sent to the AI provider.
  • Notifications. Turn off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Reset financial data. Settings → Reset data removes transactions, accounts, budgets, and goals while keeping the account.
  • Delete account. Profile → Delete Account. Deletes the account and everything linked to it — transactions, wallets, budgets, debts, goals, reports. This cannot be undone.
  • Requests. For any question about your data, including a copy or deletion request, write to r.anandamide@icloud.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Children
